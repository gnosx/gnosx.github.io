Application 6

I chose to build this system for Medtronic. I spoke with them briefly at a convention center a bit ago and was incredibly interested in their work in the medical field.

As of right now, they are targeting 3 specific fields of medical assistance: Neurological, Cardiovascular, and Diabetic, with their most infamous products being insulin pumps and heart pacemakers.

I wanted to simulate a system for a company that I'd be interested in working for and Medtronic does that for me.
